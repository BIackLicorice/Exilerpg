Abilities

How class abilities work, including casting keys, the ability bar, mana, cooldowns, damage scaling, and moving abilities between keys.

Casting

Once you have a class, you cast abilities while holding your class weapon. Each ability sits on a key:

Key Mage, Rogue, Warrior, Herald Archer
1 Right-click Sneak + start drawing your bow
2 Sneak + Right-click F (swap hands)
3 F (swap hands) Sneak + F
4 Sneak + F: your ultimate -
  • Ultimates unlock with your subclass at character level 20. The Herald is the exception: it has an ultimate from the start. An Archer's ultimate replaces Piercing Shot on Sneak + F.
  • Warriors cannot block with a shield. Their right-click always casts.
  • Each class's abilities are listed on its page: Mage, Archer, Rogue, Warrior, Herald.

The ability bar

While you hold your class weapon, the bar above your hotbar shows your mana and every key:

  • green name: ready.
  • blue name: not enough mana.
  • grey name with red seconds: on cooldown.

Out of combat, training XP pop-ups take over the bar for a moment. Then your abilities come back.

Reading an ability

Hover an ability's name on the class page. The hover shows its mana cost, its cooldown (after your cooldown reductions), its damage formula and the damage it deals with your stats right now.

Damage = base + a share of your stat levels. "8 + 50% Arcana + 30% Precision" with Arcana 30 and Precision 20 deals 8 + 15 + 6 = 29. Your total stat level counts, including bonus levels from gear, the tree, amulets and buffs. See Stats.

Ability damage: - only hits hostile mobs. Animals, villagers and other players are never hurt. - is credited to you, so kills count as yours. - is not blocked by a monster's hit cooldown, so several abilities can land on the same monster at once.

Mana

Everyone uses one mana pool:

  • Max mana = 100 + 4 per Arcana level. More comes from Deep Reserves (+30), Mana Font (+40), Steady Breath (+20) and some gear and attributes.
  • It refills 5% of your max every second (at least 1). Mana Flow and Second Breath each add +25%, Quick Recovery adds +1 a second and Mana Tide +2 a second.
  • Spending mana trains Arcana: 1 training XP per 5 mana.

Cooldowns

  • A cooldown belongs to the ability, not the key, so moving an ability never resets it.
  • Shorter cooldowns: Archmage (Arcana level 50 perk) cuts ability cooldowns by 15% and Quick Wit (Mage tree) by 10%. Some notables shorten one ability, such as Rapid Bolt, Judgment and Spring Heels.

Moving your abilities

You can put any ability you have earned on any key. Open the class page (from your character sheet) and find Your keys:

  • [change] next to a key lists your whole pool: your class's three abilities, your subclass's upgrade, your ultimate and the ultimate of a set you wear (see Sets). Abilities you haven't earned yet are greyed out and say why.
  • Picking an ability that is already on another key swaps the two. – Nothing empties a key.
  • [⟲ Default keys] puts everything back.
  • Only one ultimate (yours or a set's) can be on your keys at a time.
  • When your subclass upgrades an ability, the upgrade takes the old ability's key by default. The old one stays in your pool with its own cooldown, so you can run both.
  • You can't change keys for 10 seconds after taking damage. The page tells you how long is left.
  • Changing class or subclass puts your keys back to default.

Weapons with their own skill

A weapon with a socketed skill gem (see Gems), or one of the class signature weapons, casts its own skill on key 1 while you hold it. This is Right-click, or Sneak + draw for an Archer. Whatever you put on key 1 waits until you put that weapon away. Signature weapons have their own cooldown.

Stats, perks and nodes that help abilities

Source Effect
Arcana perks Mana Flow (+25% mana regen, level 10), Spell Echo (10% chance an ability costs no mana, level 20), Arcane Mastery (+3 damage on every ability hit, level 40), Archmage (cooldowns 15% shorter, level 50).
Arcana passives Tier I (pick one): Deep Reserves, Quick Recovery, Spell Siphon (kills restore 10 mana). Tier II (pick one): Battle Mage (melee hits restore 3 mana), Arcane Surge (+5 ability damage above 80% mana), Ley Ward.
Tree Steady Breath, Second Breath, Spellweaver (+2 damage on every ability hit), Quick Wit, Mana Font, Mana Tide, Overcharge.

The full perk and passive lists are on Stats.
