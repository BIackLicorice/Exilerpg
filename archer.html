Classes & stats

Archer

The Archer deals long, sustained damage with any bow or crossbow, moves fast, and scales with Precision and Swiftness.

Role

The Archer deals long, sustained damage with high mobility. Your normal arrows do most of the work. Your abilities add explosions, piercing shots and an escape. Precision raises all your arrow damage, and Swiftness keeps you out of reach.

Role Ranged physical damage, kiting
Casts with Any bow or crossbow (vanilla ones too)
Starter weapon Hunter’s Bow (if you don't already carry a bow)
Armour Light leather looks. Archer armour rolls less armour than a Warrior's.
Scales with (+25% training XP) Precision, Swiftness
Keys 3: Sneak + draw, F, Sneak + F
Subclasses (level 20) Sniper, Windrunner

Weapons and armour

  • Any bow or crossbow is your class weapon, from a plain vanilla bow to a rolled Exile RPG bow.
  • Archer gear rolls mostly Precision and Swiftness. It rarely rolls Might, Fortitude, Toughness or Arcana.
  • The Eagle Eye amulet has a passive that only works for Archers. See Amulets.
  • Cheap arrows: 1 cobblestone on top of 2 wood planks (any planks, in one column of the crafting grid) makes 4 arrows. The recipe appears in an Archer's recipe book.

See Weapons and Armour for the full lists.

Abilities

You have three keys. To cast your first ability, start drawing your bow while sneaking. Damage is a base number plus a share of your stat levels: "7 + 50% Precision" at Precision 30 deals 7 + 15 = 22. Hover an ability on the class page to see your numbers. See Abilities for how keys, mana and cooldowns work.

Key Ability Mana Cooldown What it does Damage
Sneak + draw Blast Arrow 15 5 s Fires one spirit arrow (30 blocks) that explodes where it lands. Everything within 3 blocks takes the damage. 7 + 50% Precision
F Disengage 15 8 s Spin up into the air, then drift down with Slow Falling. Hostile mobs within 4 blocks get Slowness II for 3 s. none
Sneak + F Piercing Shot 25 10 s An instant shot that pierces every enemy in a 30-block line. 8 + 60% Precision + 20% Swiftness

Subclasses

An Archer's subclass ultimate replaces Piercing Shot on Sneak + F.

Subclass Upgrade Ultimate (replaces Piercing Shot)
Sniper (long range) Shredding Shot replaces Blast Arrow (15 mana, 5 s): a shredding arrow through everything in a 30-block line for 8 + 55% Precision. Shredded enemies take 20% more damage for 5 s and bleed for 4 s. Deadshot (50 mana, 30 s): a devastating shot through everything in a 50-block line for 20 + 120% Precision. Slows what it hits.
Windrunner (mobility) Tailwind replaces Disengage (15 mana, 7 s): dash forward on the wind and gain Speed II for 4 s. Gale Barrage (50 mana, 30 s): for 2 s you fire a spirit arrow every quarter second where you look, each for 2 + 15% Precision + 15% Swiftness, with Speed II.

Perk tree

Every class tree has the same shape: a shared trunk and three branches, each ending in a keystone. How points, refunds and the Trades nodes work is on Skill trees. Small nodes give bonus stat levels. They work like gear bonuses: they can go past the cap, but they don't unlock perks.

"Normal arrows" means the arrows you shoot with your bow, not ability arrows.

Rain

The left branch, entered from Thick Hide. Small nodes: 7 × +2 Precision, 3 × +2 Swiftness.

Node Type Cost Effect
Barbed Arrows Notable 1 Normal arrows make what they hit bleed for 3 s (1 + 5% Precision a second).
Concussive Blast Notable 1 Blast Arrow slows what it hits (Slowness II, 3 s).
Shatter Notable 1 Monsters caught in Blast Arrow take 15% more damage from everyone for 4 s.
Arrow Storm Notable 1 Gale Barrage and Deadshot last / reach 25% more.
Rain of Ruin ★ Keystone 3 Every normal arrow hit stacks Exposed on the monster: +5% damage taken from everyone per stack, up to 3 stacks (15%), for 5 s. Your normal arrows deal 20% less.

Marksman

The middle branch, entered from Light Feet. Small nodes: 7 × +2 Precision, 3 × +2 Swiftness.

Node Type Cost Effect
Heavy Draw Notable 1 Arrows +20% damage; your bow draws 15% slower.
Headshot Notable 1 Arrow hits from 15+ blocks are criticals (+50%).
Steady Hands Notable 1 +20% arrow damage while sneaking.
Long Shot Notable 1 Piercing Shot reaches 45 blocks.
Deadeye Focus ★ Keystone 3 Piercing Shot deals double damage and slows what it hits. Blast Arrow has half the radius.

Skirmisher

The right branch, entered from Second Breath. Small nodes: 7 × +2 Swiftness, 3 × +2 Precision.

Node Type Cost Effect
Fleet Archer Notable 1 Speed I while holding a bow or crossbow.
Kiting Notable 1 Arrows shot while you are moving deal +15%.
Spring Heels Notable 1 Disengage: 2 s shorter cooldown and 2 blocks higher.
Second Wind Notable 1 Disengage gives you 2 hearts of Absorption.
Parting Shot ★ Keystone 3 After Disengage your next arrow within 5 s is charged: it explodes on impact for 8 + 60% Precision to hostile mobs within 3 blocks. Disengage costs double mana.

Good to know

  • Each Precision level gives +2% damage on arrows you shoot. It trains every time your arrows hit a mob. See Stats.
  • The Precision perks and passives (Steady Aim, Swift Shot, Hamstring, Deadeye, Eagle Eye, Sharpshooter and more) are Archer staples.
  • "Exposed" from Shatter, Rain of Ruin and Shredding Shot makes a monster take more damage from everyone, so it helps your whole party. When more than one of them applies, only the strongest counts. It is half as strong on bosses.
