Attributes

Your Personal Attribute is one random trait rolled when you first join. This page covers the four tiers, every attribute, and how to awaken or reroll yours.

What an attribute is

Every exile has one Personal Attribute: a trait rolled at random the first time you join the world. It stays with you and shapes your build. It might be extra armour, lightning on your hits, a double jump, or a drawback you learn to live with.

Don't confuse it with attribute points, the points you spend on your stats each level. The two are separate.

  • You see your attribute in the Attribute row of your character sheet (right-click your Exile’s Journal). Hover the row for the attribute card. Click it for the attribute page.
  • The card shows the base effect, the awakened form and your Awakening Deed progress bar. Many attribute numbers grow with your character level or stats, and the card shows the values you have right now. The tables below give the starting numbers.

Awakening

Every attribute has an awakened form. Your attribute keeps its base effect and adds the awakened one (sometimes it replaces a drawback). There are three ways to awaken:

  1. Complete its Awakening Deed, at any level. The deed always matches the attribute's theme, for example "Take 5000 damage" for Thick Skin.
  2. Reach character level 30. Any attribute that isn't awakened yet awakens by itself.
  3. Use an Awakening Stone. Right-click it to awaken your attribute at once, at any level.

Awakening is announced to the whole world.

Rerolling: the Orb of Fate

An Orb of Fate rerolls your attribute. Right-click it, then confirm with [Yes, reroll]. The new attribute can be of any tier. All awakening progress is lost, so think before you roll.

Awakening Stones and Orbs of Fate are very rare drops from bosses.
