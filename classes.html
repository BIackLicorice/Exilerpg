Classes

The five classes of Exile RPG, how to choose one at level 5, subclasses at level 20, and what switching costs.

Classes at a glance

A class gives you a weapon to cast with, a set of abilities on your keys, a perk tree of your own and gear that suits your playstyle. There are five classes. Each one splits into two or three subclasses later on.

Class Role Casts with Scales with Subclasses (level 20)
✧ Mage Spirit nuker: big magic damage from range Any staff or Mage Blade Arcana, Might, Precision Pyromancer, Frostbinder
➶ Archer Long, sustained damage with high mobility Any bow or crossbow Precision, Swiftness Sniper, Windrunner
✦ Rogue Utility, debuffs and high burst damage Any dagger Swiftness, Precision, Ferocity, Fortune Assassin, Poisoner, Duelist
⚔ Warrior Versatile damage dealer or tank Any sword, axe or mace Might, Fortitude, Toughness, Vitality, Steadfast Paladin, Berserker
⚑ Herald Support: heals, buffs your party, hexes enemies Herald’s Standard Arcana, Vitality, Fortune Hymnwarden, Warchanter

Choosing your class

  • Classes unlock at character level 5 (see Leveling). Until then you play without abilities.
  • Once you reach level 5, your character sheet shows [★ Choose your class]. Right-click your Exile’s Journal to open the sheet. On the class page, pick a class with [Choose] and confirm with [Yes].
  • Your first class is free.
  • Unless you already carry one of the class's weapons, you get a Common starter weapon: the Arcane Staff (Mage), Hunter’s Bow (Archer), Assassin’s Dagger (Rogue), Warblade (Warrior) or Herald’s Standard (Herald).

Subclasses

At character level 20 the class page's Subclass row unlocks. A subclass does two things:

  1. It upgrades one of your base abilities into a stronger version. For example, a Pyromancer's Mana Shield becomes Flame Shield.
  2. It gives you an ultimate. Mages, Rogues and Warriors get their ultimate on a fourth key. An Archer's ultimate replaces Piercing Shot. Heralds already have an ultimate, and a subclass swaps it for a different one.

Your first subclass is free.

Switching

Change Cost
First class free
Changing class 500 gold coins
First subclass free
Changing subclass 300 gold coins

There is no daily limit. When you change class: - your subclass is cleared, so you choose again for the new class. - every point in your old class's perk tree comes back to you for free. The new class's tree starts empty. Your Trades nodes stay (see Skill trees). - your ability keys go back to their defaults. Changing subclass resets your keys as well.

Coins are covered on Coins and bank.

What your class changes

  • Faster training for your class stats. Your class's scaling stats (the "Scales with" column above) earn +25% training XP. See Stats.
  • Abilities. You cast while holding your class weapon. Keys, mana and cooldowns work the same for every class: see Abilities.
  • A perk tree. Every class has its own tree with three build branches: see Skill trees.
  • Gear. Class gear rolls favour that class's stats. Warriors' armour rolls the most armour and Mages' the least. See Gear and Armour.
  • Amulets. Some amulets have a passive that only works for one class. See Amulets.

Unarmed and off-class weapons

Your stats (Might, perks, tree nodes, stat passives) only apply while you hold a real weapon: a sword, axe, mace, trident, bow, crossbow or Exile RPG weapon. With an empty hand or a tool you punch for exactly 1 heart. Abilities only cast while you hold a weapon of your own class.
