Coins and the bank

Gold Coins and Gold Bars - how you earn them, what they buy, how to sell, and keeping them safe in the Collector's bank.

Gold Coins and Gold Bars

The camp's money is the Gold Coin. A coin stack holds at most 99, so there's a bigger piece too:

Item Worth Stacks to
Gold Coin 1 coin 99
Gold Bar 100 coins 99 (9,900 coins in one slot)

Your purse is every coin and bar you carry. Shops count both, so you never have to break a bar yourself: when you pay, loose coins go first, then a bar is broken and the change comes back as coins. The Collector (below) changes coins into bars and back.

If your purse is short, the bank pays the rest. Buying something you can't cover with what you carry takes the difference from your bank, and a chat line tells you how much came from each.

Selling

Brenna Ironhand and the Collector use one price table, so an item sells for the same at either. Prices depend on the item: gear by its type and rarity, ingredients and gems by tier, and so on.

  • Brenna: hold the item and click [💰 Sell what you hold], or [♻ Sell all gear] to sell every piece of gear in your bag (you pick what to keep first).
  • The Collector: [💰 Sell what you hold] names a price, then [Deal] sells it. It only goes through for the exact item he priced. [🗑 Sell all junk] sells every junk item in your bags at once.

Junk items are things some monsters drop that are only worth coins ("Junk - The Collector buys it" in the tooltip). Only the Collector buys junk. He also buys many vanilla items.

He won't buy coins or bars, quest and story items, dungeon keys, the journal, the Rider's Whistle, or his own receipt.

The Collector and his bank

The Collector is a very strange trader. You meet him through the Quest Board quest The Man Who Buys Everything (from character level 4, after Warden Aldric's second job). Claiming it gives you A Receipt for Everything.

  • Right-click the receipt and your Collector appears in front of you.
  • He stays for 3 minutes, then can be called again 10 minutes after you summoned him. Right-clicking the receipt while he's out just reopens his menu.
  • He leaves early if you click [👋 Dismiss], walk 64 blocks away or log off.
  • He's yours: only you can use your Collector.

His menu shows your purse, your bank and how long he'll stay, with [💰 Sell what you hold], [🗑 Sell all junk], [🏦 Bank], [⚖ Change] and [👋 Dismiss].

The bank

[🏦 Bank] opens The Collector's Bank:

Button Does
[⬇ Deposit all] Everything in your purse goes in
[⬇ 100] Deposit 100 coins
[⬆ 100] / [⬆ 1000] Withdraw 100 or 1,000 coins
[⬆ All] Withdraw everything
  • The bank belongs to your character.
  • Banked coins can't be dropped, lost or stolen - not even when you die.
  • You only need the Collector to put coins in or take them out. Shops (Brenna, Hilde and the rest) draw on your bank automatically when your purse is short, and their menus show your bank balance.

Changing money

[⚖ Change] opens the money changer: [→ Coins to Bars] turns every 100 loose coins into a Gold Bar, and [→ Bars to Coins] turns bars back into coins.
