Getting started

Death and safety

What happens when you die, what you always keep, keeping coins safe, dungeon lives, protected areas and the no-PvP rule.

No PvP

Exile RPG is a cooperative game. Players can't hurt each other:

  • Everyone who isn't in a party is on the same peaceful team, with friendly fire off.
  • Each party is its own team, also with friendly fire off, so party members can't hurt each other either.
  • Class spells and abilities only ever hit monsters, never players, animals, villagers or mounts.

When you die

Your character never loses progress when you die. You always keep:

Kept
Character level and XP Nothing is taken away
Every stat level and its XP, prestiges, stat passives
Your class, subclass, perk tree and attribute
Your quests and their progress Except stages with a "don't die" rule (below)
Coins in the bank Banked coins can't be dropped or lost
Your mounts, titles, achievements, codex and bestiary
Your party

What you're carrying follows the world's normal Minecraft rules: if the world drops items on death, your gear, coins and bars drop where you died like any other items. That's the best reason to bank coins with the Collector. See Coins and the bank.

A few things do end when you die:

  • An active set ultimate ends, and some short-lived class effects reset.
  • Charges your attribute was building up are lost.
  • Your mount fades once nobody is riding it. Whistle it back after you respawn.
  • A quest stage with a "don't die" rule, or one that asks you to survive a stretch of time, starts over. The quest tells you when it does.

When you respawn, all your stat and gear bonuses come back on their own.

Dying in a dungeon

Dungeons have their own rules:

  • You have 3 lives per run. Each death costs one, and the party is told how many you have left.
  • After a death you wait for the party. You rejoin when they reach the next checkpoint or the boss.
  • If the whole party falls, the dungeon brings everyone back to the last checkpoint. There are no revives.
  • With no lives left you're out of that run: you can watch the party, or leave.
  • Every respawn message has a [Leave the dungeon] button.

Protected areas

Some places in the world, like camps and story sites, are protected. While you're inside one you're in adventure mode: you can't break or place blocks. A short line above your hotbar tells you when you walk in, and you're back to normal the moment you leave. Dungeons work the same way.

Staying safe out there

  • Read the level over a monster's head. Green is 3 or more levels below you, white is close, and red is 5 or more levels above you.
  • Respect coloured monsters. Magic, Rare and Champion monsters carry modifiers. An Explosive one goes off when it dies, so step back.
  • Bank your coins before a risky trip.
  • Bring friends. A party shares kill XP and quest progress, and can't hurt itself.
