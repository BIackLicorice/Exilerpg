FAQ

Quick answers to the questions new exiles ask most.

The basics

How do I open the menus? Right-click the Exile's Journal. Every menu is a set of clickable buttons in chat. See The Exile's Journal.

I lost my journal. Get back to the character sheet another way and click [My journal] for a new one. Right-click the Quest Board, click [📜 Quest Log], then [← Back] for the sheet. The [Stats] button in a level-up message works too.

The journal opened the forge instead of my sheet. Right-clicking it next to an anvil opens the forge. Sneak while you right-click to get the sheet.

An NPC's buttons do nothing. Stand closer. You need to be within about 8 blocks of Warden Aldric, Brenna Ironhand or Hilde the Stablehand for their buttons to work.

Can I turn the chat tips off? Yes. Click [? Help] on the character sheet, then [Turn tips off]. You can read every tip there at any time.

Leveling and stats

How do I level up? Use your stats. Fighting, sprinting, blocking, mining and the rest train them, and stat training feeds your character XP. Quests and bounties pay character XP directly. See Leveling.

What do I spend points on? Each character level gives one attribute point (spend it with [+1] / [+5] on the sheet) and one perk point (for your class's Perk Tree). See Stats and Skill trees.

I spent my attribute points badly. Click [↺ Respec] on the sheet, or [↺ Respec attributes] at Warden Aldric. It costs 25 Gold Coins per character level and refunds every spent point.

When do I get a class? At character level 5. Your first class is free, and changing later costs 500 coins. See Classes.

Quests

I finished a quest but got nothing. Side quests and world quests have to be claimed: open [📜 Quests] and click [★ Claim]. Story quests are turned in to Warden Aldric with [★ Turn in]. See Quests.

Some of my items vanished when I took a quest. Some quests take the items they ask for the moment that stage is complete, even right as you accept. Chat tells you when that happens.

How many quests can I carry? Up to 85 side quests, plus your story quest.

Where do I find bounties? Two places: the Quest Board (3 a day and 1 a week, with bonus pay) and Warden Aldric's Bounty Board (3 a day, after his second story quest).

How do I find where a quest wants me to go? Click [Track] next to it in the quest log. An arrow and the distance show above your hotbar.

Coins

How do I keep my coins safe? Put them in the Collector's bank. Banked coins can't be dropped or lost, and shops use them automatically when your purse is short. You meet the Collector through the Quest Board quest The Man Who Buys Everything. See Coins and the bank.

My coins don't fit in one stack. A Gold Bar is worth 100 coins. The Collector's [⚖ Change] turns 100 coins into a bar, and shops accept bars as they are.

Where do I sell gear? Brenna Ironhand ([💰 Sell what you hold], [♻ Sell all gear]) or the Collector. Both pay the same prices.

Gear

My new weapon does no damage. Gear only works when its level is within range of yours. Check the item's level. See Gear.

My helmet in my hand gives nothing. Gear only counts in its own slot.

Friends and parties

Can other players hurt me? No. Players outside a party can't hurt each other, and party members can't hurt each other. See Death and safety.

Do we share XP in a party? Yes. Kill XP is shared with members within 200 blocks (in full within 30 blocks), and quest progress is shared wherever members are. See Parties.

Who opens the dungeon in a party? Only the party leader can start a run with a key. Far-away members get a [Join] button.

Mounts

How do I get a mount? Finish Hilde's First Mount on the Quest Board (from level 10), then buy Dustmane (600 coins) or Ironhide (2,500) from Hilde the Stablehand. See Mounts.

My mount disappeared. A mount nobody rides fades after a few seconds. Right-click the Rider's Whistle to call it back.

Can I bring my mount into a dungeon? No. Entering a dungeon sets you down.

Dying

What do I lose when I die? No character progress at all. Your levels, stats, quests, mounts and banked coins are safe. Items you carry follow the world's normal death rules. See Death and safety.
