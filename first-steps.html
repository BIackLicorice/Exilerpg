Getting started

First steps

Your first hour in Exile RPG, step by step - from the journal to your class at level 5.

New to the camp? Follow these steps in order. None of them is a trap: you can't lock yourself out of anything by doing them in a different order.

1. Open the Exile's Journal

Every new character gets an Exile's Journal (a glowing book). Right-click it to open your character sheet in chat. Almost everything in Exile RPG starts from here: your stats, quests, party, class, perk tree and help pages.

Lost it? Reach the sheet another way (the Quest Board's [📜 Quest Log], then [← Back]) and click [My journal] for a new one. See The Exile's Journal.

2. Talk to Warden Aldric

Warden Aldric keeps the Exile camp. Right-click him to see his menu. He gives you the story quests, one at a time: click [✔ Accept], do the job, then come back and click [★ Turn in]. His first job is simple - go out past the fires and kill some monsters.

While you're at the camp, meet the others:

Who What they do
Warden Aldric Story quests, his daily Bounty Board (after his second job), attribute respec
The Quest Board Side quests and the daily and weekly bounties
Brenna Ironhand Sells forge materials, buys your gear, counts as an anvil for the forge
Hilde the Stablehand Mounts, once you've earned her trust

You need to stand within about 8 blocks of a person for their menu buttons to work.

3. Fight, and let your stats train

You don't pick stats from a list - they level by doing. Hitting things trains Might, taking hits trains Vitality, sprinting trains Swiftness, mining trains Mining, and so on. A popup above your hotbar shows which stat just gained XP. The full list is on the Stats page.

Every stat level-up also gives you character XP, and so does most of the training XP you earn. See Leveling.

4. Spend your points

Each character level gives you:

  • 1 attribute point - spend it on the sheet with [+1] or [+5] next to a stat. Each point adds one level to that stat.
  • 1 perk point - for your class's Perk Tree. The points wait for you until you pick a class.

A title on screen and a chat line with [Stats] and [Passive Tree] buttons tell you when you level up.

5. Pick up coins

Monsters drop Gold Coins, and Magic and better monsters drop more. Coins pay for almost everything: materials, mounts, respecs and class changes. See Coins and the bank.

6. Watch for coloured monsters

Most monsters are plain, but some are Magic, Rare or even Champions. They're tougher, carry extra modifiers (Hasted, Armored, Vampiric, Explosive and more), and drop better loot. The level over a monster's head is green if it's 3 or more levels below you, white if it's close, and red if it's 5 or more levels above you.

Monsters are levelled to the players near them when they appear, so the world grows with you.

7. Choose a class at level 5

At character level 5 a [★ Choose your class] button appears on your sheet. Pick Mage, Archer, Rogue, Warrior or Herald. Your first class is free. See Classes.

8. Keep going

  • Take side quests from the Quest Board and claim them in your quest log - see Quests.
  • Equip better gear as it drops.
  • Team up with friends in a party: kills and quest progress are shared.
  • At level 10 you can work toward your first mount.
  • At level 20 your class opens its subclasses.

Tips as you go

The first time you meet something new (your first orb, your first dungeon key, your first party), a short tip appears in chat. Each tip shows once. To read them all again, click [? Help] on the character sheet. You can turn tips off or back on from that page.
