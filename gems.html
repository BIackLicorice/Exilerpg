Gear & forge

Gems

Forge gems (Ruby to Topaz) and the stat each one rolls per tier, plus the six skill gems you can set into a weapon's skill socket.

Exile RPG has two kinds of gems:

  • Forge gems (Ruby, Jade, Moonstone...) go into a piece at the forge and become its stat affixes.
  • Skill gems (Starfall Volley, Riftblade...) go into a weapon's skill socket and give it an ability.

Forge gems

There is one gem for each combat stat, in three tiers (✦, ✦✦, ✦✦✦). Put a gem into a piece at the forge and it becomes an affix of its stat. Its tier decides the range the affix rolls in:

Gem Stat ✦ ✦✦ ✦✦✦

Garnet Vitality +1 to +4 +4 to +7 +7 to +10
Amber Ferocity +1 to +4 +4 to +7 +7 to +10
Jade Precision +2 to +6 +6 to +10 +10 to +15
Moonstone Arcana +1 to +3 +3 to +5 +5 to +8
Opal Fortitude +1 to +3 +3 to +5 +5 to +8
Onyx Toughness +1 to +3 +3 to +5 +5 to +8
Sapphire Swiftness +1 to +3 +3 to +5 +5 to +8
Aquamarine Steadfast +1 to +4 +4 to +7 +7 to +10
Topaz Fortune +1 to +3 +3 to +5 +5 to +8

How forge gems behave:

  • Gems of one stat add up into one affix line. Three ✦✦ Rubies make one Might affix rolling +9 to +15. All of them share one affix slot.
  • The metal rolls them. Every gem affix is rolled several times and the best roll stays. The count is the metal's rank (copper 1 up to netherite 5), plus 1 for each tier your ingredients average above ✦, plus bonuses from modifiers and perks. See forge.
  • Gem affixes are fixed numbers. They don't grow with the piece's level.
  • Garnet (Vitality) can't go into a weapon. Weapons never carry Vitality.
  • The Blacksmithing passive Gem Setter makes every gem count one tier higher (✦✦✦ is the top).

Where forge gems come from

  • Mining. Each ore you mine has a 2.5% chance of a modifier from the ore list, and each stone-type block a 0.3% chance from the stone list. Gems are on both lists. The gem's tier follows your Mining level: ✦ from level 1, ✦✦ from 15, ✦✦✦ from 30, and one tier higher 5% of the time.
  • The Mining passive Gem Sense makes these finds half again as common, and the Prospector Dust armour modifier adds more (see ingredients).
  • Brenna Ironhand sometimes stocks a ✦✦ gem among her daily materials.
  • Combine: at the forge, 9 gems of one tier make 1 of the next tier (9 ✦ = 1 ✦✦, 9 ✦✦ = 1 ✦✦✦).

Skill gems

A skill gem is an ability you can put into a weapon. Each gem is made for one weapon type, but it fits any weapon with a skill socket and works the same in all of them.

Skill sockets

A skill socket is one empty slot on a weapon:

  • Extraordinary and God's Tool weapons always have one, including every relic.
  • At the forge, a Skill Socket Stone (any piece) or a Void Crown Fragment (helmets) adds one to the piece you make.

Setting a gem

Drop the weapon on a crafting table, then drop the gem on it, the same way you use an orb. A socket that already holds a gem refuses another.

Casting

While you hold the weapon, the gem takes your first ability key. On your own class's weapon you cast it like your first ability. On any other weapon (or with no class), right-click casts it, or sneak + draw on a bow. Each gem has its own mana cost and its own cooldown, separate from your class abilities. Its damage scales with the stats it lists, whatever your class or weapon.

Where skill gems come from

Skill gems drop from bosses. Bigger bosses give each player nearby their own chance at one.
