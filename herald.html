Classes & stats

Herald

The Herald is the support class, with a small healing hymn, banners and anthems that buff your party, and hexes that weaken enemies, scaling with Arcana, Vitality and Fortune.

Role

The Herald is the support class. It has one small heal. The rest of the kit raises your party and breaks your enemies: banners and anthems give nearby players real stat levels, and hexes strip enemies' armour and slow them. The Herald is the only class that has its ultimate from the start. A subclass swaps it for a different one.

Role Support: party buffs, enemy debuffs, a small heal
Casts with A Standard (the Herald’s Standard and other rolled Standards)
Starter weapon Herald’s Standard
Armour Gold-dyed leather looks. Herald armour rolls slightly less armour than a Warrior's (×0.95).
Scales with (+25% training XP) Arcana, Vitality, Fortune
Keys 4: Right-click, Sneak + Right-click, F, Sneak + F
Subclasses (level 20) Hymnwarden, Warchanter

Weapons and armour

  • Standards are the Herald's class weapon. They come in many looks, from camp banners to bell poles. Only a Standard casts Herald abilities.
  • Herald gear rolls mostly Arcana, Vitality and Fortune. It rarely rolls Might, Ferocity or Precision.

See Weapons and Armour for the full lists.

Abilities

Buffs from banners and anthems are stat levels: "+6 Might" works exactly like 6 extra levels of Might while it lasts. Buffs reach every player in range, including you and your party. Hover an ability on the class page to see your numbers. See Abilities for how keys, mana and cooldowns work.

Key Ability Mana Cooldown What it does
Right-click Mending Hymn 20 8 s Heals you and every player within 6 blocks for 4 + 10% Vitality health and gives Regeneration I for 3 s. Also cleanses Poison and Wither.
Sneak + Right-click Hex of Frailty 20 10 s Curses the spot you look at (24 blocks). Enemies within 4 blocks take 3 + 25% Arcana + 15% Fortune damage, and for 6 s get Weakness, Slowness, Glowing and -5 armour.
F Rallying Banner 25 20 s Plant your banner for 10 s. Every player within 8 blocks of it is Rallied: +6 Might, +6 Precision, +6 Arcana and Speed I. The buff stays for 1 s after you step away.
Sneak + F Anthem of the Stranded (ultimate) 50 40 s A 10 s anthem that moves with you (10 blocks). Players get Fortitude and Toughness, each 6 + 15% Arcana + 5% Vitality of yours (at most 20), plus +8 Steadfast and +4 Might, Precision, Arcana and Swiftness. Enemies in it get Slowness II, Weakness, Glowing and -5 armour.

Subclasses

Subclass Upgrade New ultimate (Sneak + F)
Hymnwarden (protector) Warding Banner replaces Rallying Banner (25 mana, 20 s): plant a warding banner for 10 s. Players within 8 blocks get +8 Fortitude, +8 Steadfast and +4 Vitality, and 2 hearts of Absorption when it is planted. Aegis Chorus (50 mana, 40 s): an 8 s chorus around you (10 blocks). Players get Resistance I and Fortitude and Toughness, each 10 + 25% Arcana + 10% Vitality of yours (at most 30). Every second it strips Poison, Wither, Slowness, Weakness, Blindness, Darkness and Hunger.
Warchanter (aggressor) Curse of Ruin replaces Hex of Frailty (22 mana, 10 s): curse the spot you look at (24 blocks). Enemies within 5 blocks take 5 + 35% Arcana + 20% Fortune and for 6 s get Wither I, Weakness II, Slowness II, Glowing and -8 armour. Battle Hymn (50 mana, 40 s): an 8 s war song around you (10 blocks). Players get +8 Might, +8 Precision, +8 Arcana, +6 Ferocity, Strength I and Speed I. Enemies in it get Weakness, Glowing and -5 armour.

Mending Hymn stays your only heal in both subclasses.

Perk tree

Every class tree has the same shape: a shared trunk and three branches, each ending in a keystone. How points, refunds and the Trades nodes work is on Skill trees. Small nodes give bonus stat levels. They work like gear bonuses: they can go past the cap, but they don't unlock perks.

Choir

The left branch, entered from Thick Hide. Small nodes: 7 × +2 Vitality, 3 × +2 Arcana.

Node Type Cost Effect
Mana Font Notable 1 +40 max mana.
Long Hymn Notable 1 Mending Hymn heals +2.
Wide Banner Notable 1 Rallying Banner reaches 12 blocks (was 8).
Encore Notable 1 Anthem of the Stranded lasts 14 s (was 10).
Chorus of the Stranded ★ Keystone 3 Your banners and anthems buff you twice as much. Hex of Frailty does no damage.

Cursebinder

The middle branch, entered from Light Feet. Small nodes: 7 × +2 Arcana, 3 × +2 Fortune.

Node Type Cost Effect
Spellweaver Notable 1 +2 damage on every ability hit.
Deep Hex Notable 1 Hex of Frailty lasts 8 s (was 6).
Spreading Rot Notable 1 Curse of Ruin's radius is 7 (was 5).
Mark of Ruin Notable 1 Hexed mobs take +10% damage from everyone.
Dirge ★ Keystone 3 Every hex also deals 2 + 15% Arcana a second while it lasts. Mending Hymn heals half.

Standard-Bearer

The right branch, entered from Second Breath. Small nodes: 7 × +2 Vitality, 3 × +2 Fortune.

Node Type Cost Effect
Lifeblood Notable 1 +6 max health.
Shield Master Notable 1 A monster's hit on your shield heals you 1 heart.
Mana Tide Notable 1 +2 mana per second.
Rally Notable 1 Planting a banner gives every player in it 1 heart of Absorption.
Last Stand ★ Keystone 3 An ally within 8 blocks who falls under 20% health gets Resistance II for 3 s (once a minute each). -15% move speed.

Good to know

  • Encore also lengthens Aegis Chorus and Battle Hymn (8 s to 12 s), and Deep Hex also lengthens Curse of Ruin.
  • Rally, Last Stand and every banner and anthem help the players around you, so a Herald shines in a party.
  • The buffs show on your stats while they last, because they are real stat levels: armour, damage and health move with them.
  • Vitality (your heal and anthems) trains when you take damage and survive. Arcana trains as you spend mana. Fortune trains from fresh loot chests, trades and enchanting. See Stats.
