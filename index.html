Welcome, Stranded

Exile RPG turns Minecraft Java into a full role-playing game. You wake up as an exile at a small camp on the edge of a hostile world. From there you:

  • train your stats by using them - fighting, sprinting, blocking, mining, fishing and more,
  • climb character levels, choose a class and grow it through its perk tree,
  • hunt Magic, Rare and Champion monsters for rolled gear, orbs, gems and coins,
  • take on Warden Aldric's story, the Quest Board and daily bounties,
  • forge your own gear, ride mounts, earn titles, and team up in a party.

Everything runs through the Exile's Journal and clickable chat menus. You never need to type a command.
