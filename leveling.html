Getting started

Leveling

How your character level works - where character XP comes from, how much each level needs, and what levels give you.

Exile RPG has two kinds of level:

  • Stat levels - each of your stats (Might, Vitality, Mining...) levels on its own by being used. Stats cap at 50. That's all on the Stats page.
  • Your character level - one number for your whole character, shown at the top of the character sheet. This page is about that one.

Where character XP comes from

You never earn character XP directly from killing a monster. It comes from your stats and from the work you do:

Source What you get
A stat levels up Character XP equal to the stat's new level. Mining going from 14 to 15 gives 15 character XP
Training XP A big share (half, counted before the training slow-down) of the training XP your non-gathering stats earn also counts as character XP. Gathering stats (Mining, Logging, Farming, Fishing, Cooking) and Blacksmithing give none of this share
A stat at its cap (50) It keeps paying a smaller share (10%) of what it would have earned, so maxed stats still help you level
Quests Many quests pay character XP directly, and story and world quests pay a lot. See Quests
Bounties The Quest Board's daily and weekly bounties pay a bonus of character XP on top
Dungeons and world events Finishing them pays character XP
Party kills Kill XP your party shares with you trains your stats, and that counts too. See Parties

Your character level is checked every few seconds, so XP from any source levels you up. If one big reward covers several levels, you climb them all at once.

Prestige makes a stat train faster, but it doesn't increase the character XP share. See Prestige.

How much XP each level needs

The XP for your next level is 125 + 25 × your current level. It grows steadily:

From level XP to the next level Total XP to reach that level
1 150 -
5 250 750
10 375 2,250
15 500 4,375
20 625 7,125
30 875 14,500
40 1,125 24,375
50 1,375 36,750

There's no hard cap on character level.

What every level gives

Each character level gives you:

  • +1 attribute point - spend it on the sheet with [+1] / [+5]. Each point adds a level to a stat, up to its cap.
  • +1 perk point - spend it on your class's Perk Tree (Skill trees). Points earned before you have a class wait for you.

A title on screen, a sound and a chat line with [Stats] and [Passive Tree] buttons mark the moment.

Milestones

Level What opens
5 Choose your class (the first one is free) - Classes
10 The quest that leads to your first mount - Mounts
20 Your class's subclasses - Classes; the title The Seasoned
40 The title The Veteran - Titles

Many quests on the Quest Board also need a minimum character level before they show up.

Your level and the world

  • Monsters scale to you. A monster's level is set when it appears, from the players nearby (a little below to a few above their level). Some places have a minimum monster level. A level shown in red over a monster's head means it's 5 or more levels above you.
  • Gear has a level. A piece only works for you while its level is within range of yours. See Gear.
  • Bounty coins grow with you. Warden Aldric's bounties pay 2% more coins for every character level.
  • Respecs cost more as you grow. An attribute respec or a perk tree reset costs 25 Gold Coins per character level.
