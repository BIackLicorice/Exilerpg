Classes & stats

Mage

The Mage is a spirit nuker who deals big magic damage from range with a staff or Mage Blade and scales with Arcana.

Role

The Mage is a spirit nuker. You fire bolts and beams from range, protect yourself with a mana shield, and at level 20 add fire or ice. Most of your damage comes from Arcana, which also raises your max mana. Might and Precision add to some spells.

Role Ranged magic damage
Casts with Any staff or Mage Blade
Starter weapon Arcane Staff
Armour Light: robes and soft leather looks. Mage armour rolls the least armour of any class.
Scales with (+25% training XP) Arcana, Might, Precision
Keys 4: Right-click, Sneak + Right-click, F, Sneak + F
Subclasses (level 20) Pyromancer, Frostbinder

Weapons and armour

  • Staffs are the classic Mage weapon. A Mage Blade is a light blade that also casts your spells and hits harder in melee than a staff. Both count as your class weapon, so you can cast with either. Their affixes lean towards Arcana.
  • Mage gear rolls mostly Arcana, then Vitality and Precision. It rarely rolls Ferocity, Fortitude, Toughness or Steadfast.
  • The Forbidden Amulet has a passive that only works for Mages. See Amulets.

See Weapons and Armour for the full lists.

Abilities

Damage is a base number plus a share of your stat levels. For example, "6 + 40% Arcana" at Arcana 30 deals 6 + 12 = 18. To see your current numbers, hover an ability on the class page. See Abilities for how keys, mana and cooldowns work.

Key Ability Mana Cooldown What it does Damage
Right-click Arcane Bolt 10 0.6 s A fast bolt of spirit energy (24 blocks). 6 + 40% Arcana
Sneak + Right-click Spirit Lance 25 4 s A beam that pierces every enemy in a 14-block line. 8 + 50% Arcana + 30% Precision
F Mana Shield 20 15 s For 6 s nothing gets through. Every hit drains 2 mana per point of damage it would have done, and the shield breaks when your mana runs out. none
Sneak + F Ultimate Unlocks with your subclass.

Subclasses

Subclass Upgrade (F) Ultimate (Sneak + F)
Pyromancer (fire) Flame Shield (20 mana, 15 s): a Mana Shield that also gives Fire Resistance. Hostile mobs that hit you catch fire and take 2 + 15% Arcana + 15% Might. Meteor (60 mana, 40 s): calls a meteor down where you look (30 blocks). It lands 1 s later and explodes in a 5-block radius for 20 + 100% Arcana + 50% Might, setting enemies on fire.
Frostbinder (ice and control) Ice Barrier (20 mana, 15 s): a Mana Shield. While it holds, hostile mobs within 4 blocks get Slowness II. Blizzard (60 mana, 40 s): a 6 s ice storm where you look, radius 6. It deals 2 + 10% Arcana every half second and applies Slowness III.

Perk tree

Every class tree has the same shape: a shared trunk and three branches, each ending in a keystone. How points, refunds and the Trades nodes work is on Skill trees. Small nodes give bonus stat levels. They work like gear bonuses: they can go past the cap, but they don't unlock perks.

Battlemage

The left branch, entered from Thick Hide. Small nodes: 7 × +2 Arcana, 3 × +2 Precision.

Node Type Cost Effect
Rapid Bolt Notable 1 Arcane Bolt cooldown 0.4 s (was 0.6).
Split Bolt Notable 1 Arcane Bolt fires two bolts at 60% each.
Spellweaver Notable 1 +2 damage on every ability hit.
Chain Notable 1 A kill with Arcane Bolt refunds its mana.
Arcane Barrage ★ Keystone 3 Arcane Bolt fires THREE bolts at 45% each. Spirit Lance costs double mana.

Elementalist

The middle branch, entered from Light Feet. Small nodes: 7 × +2 Arcana, 3 × +1 Might.

Node Type Cost Effect
Mana Font Notable 1 +40 max mana.
Quick Wit Notable 1 Ability cooldowns are 10% shorter.
Overcharge Notable 1 All ability stat ratios count 25% higher. -15% max health.
Long Lance Notable 1 Spirit Lance reaches 20 blocks (was 14).
Overcharge Mastery ★ Keystone 3 All ability stat ratios count 50% higher. -30% max health. Arcane Bolt costs 20 mana.

Warden

The right branch, entered from Second Breath. Small nodes: 7 × +2 Arcana, 3 × +1 Might.

Node Type Cost Effect
Mana Tide Notable 1 +2 mana per second.
Thick Shield Notable 1 Mana Shield (and its upgrades) last 8 s (was 6).
Reflect Notable 1 Your shield returns 25% of what it stopped to the attacker.
Blood Pact Notable 1 Below 20 mana your abilities cost health instead (1 per 5 mana).
Blood Magic ★ Keystone 3 Abilities cost health instead of mana: 1 health per 5 mana. Mana Shield becomes a Health Shield that costs 10 health.

Good to know

  • Mana is 100 + 4 per Arcana level and refills 5% of your max every second. Arcana trains as you spend mana (and when you enchant), so casting a lot makes you stronger. See Stats.
  • The Arcana perks and passives on the Stats page are mostly Mage tools: Mana Flow, Spell Echo, Arcane Mastery, Archmage, Deep Reserves, Arcane Surge and more.
  • Each branch has a different focus. Battlemage builds around Arcane Bolt. Elementalist raises every ability's damage but costs you health. Warden builds around your shield and mana. With Blood Magic you cast with health instead of mana, so it pairs well with Vitality and healing.
