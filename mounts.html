Getting started

Mounts

Getting your first mount from Hilde the Stablehand, using the Rider's Whistle, your stable, and mount upgrades.

Mounts carry you across the world much faster than your feet. You own mounts and keep them forever, and you call whichever one you've equipped with a single item: the Rider's Whistle.

Your first mount: Hilde the Stablehand

Hilde the Stablehand runs the stable. She doesn't sell mounts to strangers. First you have to finish the Quest Board quest Hilde's First Mount:

  • It appears on the board from character level 10, once you're far enough into Warden Aldric's story.
  • She asks for 24 Wheat (feed) and 6 Leather (tack), then a hand at the stable (talk to her).
  • It pays 150 coins and some character XP, and from then on Hilde will sell to you.

Until then her menu reminds you what she's waiting for. The Rider's Whistle is always for sale.

What Hilde sells

Mount Price What it's like Attack
Dustmane 600 coins A steady camp horse: quick and reliable None
Ironhide 2,500 coins A war bear: slower than a horse, with twice the health Slash
Rider's Whistle 500 coins A spare whistle -

Your first mount comes with a whistle for free. Her menu shows your purse and bank together, and a purchase draws on your bank if your purse is short. The rarer mounts aren't hers to sell: they're won elsewhere in the world.

The Rider's Whistle

One whistle calls every mount you own. It works from your main hand or your off hand.

What you do What happens
Right-click on foot Calls your equipped mount and puts you in the saddle
Sneak + right-click on foot Opens Your Stable
Right-click in the saddle Your mount's attack, if it has one
Sneak in the saddle Get off. The mount fades about 3 seconds later
  • Only you can ride your called mount. Anyone else who climbs on is put straight back down.
  • A mount nobody is riding fades after a few seconds. You can always whistle it back.
  • Mounts can be hurt and killed. A fallen mount isn't lost - just call it again.
  • Mounts can't follow you into a dungeon. Entering one sets you down.
  • Your spells and abilities never hit mounts.

Your Stable

Sneak + right-click the whistle to open Your Stable. It lists every mount you own:

  • [Equip] picks which mount the whistle calls.
  • speed and attack show each mount's upgrade levels (0-5), each with a [+15% · cost] button to buy the next level.

Upgrades

Each mount upgrades separately. Every level adds +15%, so five levels is +75%:

Level 1 2 3 4 5
Cost (Gold Coins) 500 1,200 2,500 5,000 10,000
  • Speed - every mount. Takes effect immediately, even on the mount you're sitting on.
  • Attack - only mounts with an attack. Raises that attack's damage.

Ironhide's Slash

Ironhide swipes a heavy blow through everything just ahead of it. It has a 3-second cooldown and hits every monster in front of the bear.
