Gear & forge

Orbs

The crafting orbs - what Transmutation, Regal, Chaos and Scribing do, how to use them on a crafting table, where they drop, and their use on dungeon keys.

Using an orb

Orbs change the affixes and rarity of gear you already have. You use them on a crafting table, without any menu:

  1. Drop the item (Q) so it lands on top of a crafting table.
  2. Drop the orb on the same table, right next to the item.

The orb works on the item within a moment and is used up. The item pops back up, rebuilt, for you to pick up. If the orb doesn't fit the item, a red line in chat tells you why and the orb is not spent. Pick both up and try again.

  • Only a freshly dropped orb works (within about 3 seconds of landing), and one orb works per drop.
  • An orb never changes a piece's item level or made-for class. It works on the affixes, sockets and rarity. When the rarity goes up, the base numbers rise to the new rarity, and the piece remembers where in the quality window it rolled, so an upgrade never makes it worse.
  • Messages go only to the player who threw the orb.
  • Each orb leaves its mark at the bottom of the tooltip: ◈ Transmutation, ✦ Regal, ☄ Chaos, ✎ Scribing. Chaos also counts how many times the piece was rerolled.

Affixes need open sockets (◇). See rarity for how many sockets each rarity can hold.

The four orbs

Orb Works on What it does
Orb of Transmutation a Common item with an open socket It becomes Uncommon with 1-2 affixes (as many as it has open sockets). A one-socket item gets a second, open socket.
Regal Orb an Uncommon item with an open socket It becomes Rare. It keeps its affixes and adds one more.
Chaos Orb a Rare item with affixes It rerolls every affix into a new set, with the same number of affixes.
Orb of Scribing any item that can hold affixes It wipes every added affix. The sockets stay, now open. A set piece's or unique's own fixed stats stay too.

New affixes follow the same rules as drops. They lean toward the piece's made-for class, Rare and up can roll any combat stat, and weapons never roll Vitality.

Extraordinary and God's Tool gear can't be crafted on. Set pieces and uniques keep their own rarity, so of these four only Scribing works on them.

Orb Share

Brenna Ironhand and The Collector also buy orbs (see coins and bank).

Sockets and single affixes

Adding sockets and single affixes is the forge's job. Void Pearls and Shulker Plates give a forged piece extra sockets. Orbs of Sockets and Orbs of Affixing still exist and still work if you come across one, but they are not part of monster loot. If an orb tells you an item "has no open socket", that item can't take more affixes from orbs. Look for a drop with more open sockets, or forge one.

Orbs on dungeon keys

Two orbs also work on dungeon keys, in the same way on a crafting table:

  • Regal Orb: the key goes up one tier (up to tier 5).
  • Chaos Orb: rerolls the key's modifiers.

Skill gems

Skill gems are set into a weapon's skill socket the same way: drop the weapon on a crafting table, then drop the gem on it.
