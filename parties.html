Parties

Making a party, inviting and managing members, the Party tab, and what a party shares - kill XP, quest progress and dungeon runs.

A party is a group you make on purpose. It has no size limit and lasts across logouts: you stay in it until you leave, are kicked, or it's disbanded.

Opening the Party tab

Click [👥 Party] in the footer of the character sheet.

  • Not in a party yet? Click [+ Create party] to start one with you as leader, or [⚙ Invite people]. Inviting someone also creates the party.
  • In a party, the tab shows one member at a time (see below).

Running a party

Action Who How
Invite The leader [⚙ Manage] lists everyone online who isn't in your party, nearest first, 10 to a page ([More ▶]). Click [+ Invite]
Accept / Decline The invited player [Accept] or [Decline] on the invite. It lasts 60 seconds. Accepting while you're in another party moves you over
Leave Anyone [✖ Leave], then [Yes, leave]
Kick The leader [⚙ Manage] > [✖ Kick], then [Yes, kick]. Works on offline members too, and they're told when they next log in
Make leader The leader [⚙ Manage] > [★ Make leader] (online members only)
Disband The leader [⚙ Manage] > [Disband], then [Yes, disband]
  • A player can hold only one invite at a time. A newer one replaces it.
  • If the leader leaves, the member who joined earliest takes over (an online one first).
  • A leader who hasn't been online for a full in-game day loses the lead to the earliest online member.
  • When the last member leaves, the party ends.
  • Members see [⚙ Members] instead of [⚙ Manage]: the member list and [✖ Leave].

The Party tab

The tab fits one member on one chat screen, with a strip of every member at the top. Click a name in the strip, or use [◀ Prev] / [Next ▶], to switch. You're in the strip too, so you can see what your party sees of you.

  • Left: everything they're wearing and holding - main hand, off hand, armour and the three amulet slots. Hover an item for its full tooltip.
  • Middle: level and XP bar, class and subclass, ultimate, health, mana, unspent points and tokens, prestiges, where they are and the day they joined.
  • Right: their stats with short names (VIT, MIG, FER...), each with its level, gear bonus and prestige stars.
  • Bottom lines: set pieces and set ultimate, mount, titles and wings. Hover them for details.

In the strip, ★ marks the leader and ○ an offline member. An offline member's page shows what the server last knew about them and when they were last seen. [↻] reads their gear again.

What a party shares

No friendly fire

Party members can't hurt each other with melee or arrows, and class spells only ever hit monsters. Members also see each other faintly while invisible. Players outside any party can't hurt each other either - see Death and safety.

Party chat

The party is a Minecraft team, so Minecraft's team chat (/teammsg, or /tm) talks only to your party.

Kill XP

A kill trains 5 Ferocity XP for the killer. In a party, every other member within 200 blocks gets that XP too:

Where the member is They get The killer keeps
Within 30 blocks of the kill The full kill XP All of it
30-200 blocks away 25% 75% (when at least one member is that far out)

On top of that, a big level gap cuts a member's share:

Level difference from the killer Share
0-5 100%
6-10 50%
11-20 20%
21+ 5%

Small shares add up over many kills. Shared XP trains Ferocity like any other XP, so it also gives character XP.

Quest progress

Party members carrying the same quest share its progress, wherever they are: kills, zones and places reached, spots found, people talked to and encounters finished. Item hand-ins and collecting stay your own. Loot, coins and keys from a kill stay with the killer.

Members on the death screen don't receive kill XP or quest kills.

Dungeons

In a party, only the leader starts a dungeon run (using their key). Members near the entrance come in with them. Members who are too far away, or in another world, get a [Join] button for 60 seconds that teleports them into the run. A key used by a non-leader is refused with a message.

Common questions

  • Does my party survive a logout or a server restart? Yes.
  • Is there a size cap? No.
  • Can I see a member's gear while they're offline? Yes, as it was when the server last saw it.
