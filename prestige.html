Prestige

Prestige a stat at level 50 to reset it to 0 in exchange for permanently faster training and a Legendary token.

What prestige is

When a stat reaches the cap of 50, you can prestige it. The stat goes back to level 0, and from then on it trains much faster. Prestige is how you keep a stat growing after the cap, and how you show you've mastered it.

How to prestige

  1. Get the stat to level 50 from trained levels and spent attribute points. Bonus levels from gear, sets, amulets and your perk tree don't count.
  2. On the character sheet the stat's row shows MAX [✦]. You can also open the stat's page and click [Prestige ✦].
  3. Confirm with [Yes, prestige].

What happens

The stat Goes back to level 0 and its XP is cleared.
Spent attribute points Refunded, so you can spend them again anywhere.
Perks That stat's perks have to be earned again as it levels back up.
Passive choices That stat's Tier I and Tier II choices are cleared. Choose again at levels 15 and 35.
Training speed +100% training XP for that stat, stacking: ×2 after one prestige, ×3 after two, ×4 after three, and so on.
Reward +1 Legendary token. Your character sheet shows how many you have.

Bonus levels from gear and the tree stay. They still add to the stat's effect while it climbs back up.

Your sheet shows each stat's prestiges as ✦, and the [✚ Bonuses] screen lists every prestige with its XP multiplier.

Should you prestige?

  • Your character level isn't slowed down. The share of training XP that feeds your character level is worked out before the prestige multiplier. Prestiging doesn't speed up character levels, and it doesn't slow them either.
  • A capped stat still helps your character level a little. A stat at 50 earns no stat XP, but it still pays a small share (10%) of what it would have earned towards your character level.
  • You lose power for a while. Until it climbs back, the prestiged stat gives less, and its perks are gone. Prestige when you can afford the dip: between big fights, or when your gear bonus levels carry the stat.
  • Faster every time. Each prestige makes the next climb quicker, so a stat you use all the time (Might for a Warrior, Arcana for a Mage, Mining for a miner) climbs back fastest.

See Stats for how each stat trains, and Leveling for character levels.
