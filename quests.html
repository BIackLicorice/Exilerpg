Getting started

Quests

How quests work - Warden Aldric's story, the Quest Board, both kinds of bounty, the quest log, tracking and claiming.

Quests come from four places:

Where What Where you finish it
Warden Aldric The story, one quest at a time Turn it in to Aldric
The Quest Board Side quests, plus daily and weekly bounties Claim it in your quest log
Aldric's Bounty Board Three quick bounties a day Turn them in to Aldric
People and things in the world World quests Claim them in your quest log

The story: Warden Aldric

Right-click Warden Aldric at the Exile camp. His menu shows your current story quest:

  • [✔ Accept] takes it. Hover the button first to see every stage and the reward.
  • While you're working on it, his menu shows your progress, for example Slay 10 hostile monsters (4/10).
  • When it's done, come back and click [★ Turn in] for the reward.

He offers story quests one at a time, in order. Some need a minimum character level, and they never count against your quest limit. When you've done everything he has for now, he says so and points you to the board.

The Quest Board

The Quest Board at the camp lists every side quest you can take right now: your level is high enough, you've done anything it needs first, and you're not already carrying it. Quests you've finished only come back if they're repeatable (marked ↻).

  • Tabs along the top: [All] [Story] [Combat] [Gathering] [Crafting]. The board and your quest log share the tab you picked.
  • Six quests to a page. Use [← Prev] / [Next →].
  • Hover a quest to see its stages, what it needs and its reward. Click [✔ Accept] to take it.
  • [📜 Quest Log] jumps to your log, and [↻] re-reads the board.

There's no claim button on the board. Finished side quests are claimed in your quest log.

Quest Board bounties

At the top of the board you'll see bounties picked just for you from the work you can take:

How many Resets Bonus when claimed
Today's bounties 3 every day of server time +25 coins, +150 character XP
This week 1 every week of server time +120 coins, +600 character XP, and a 35% chance of a dungeon key

Click [Take] to accept one like any other quest. Claim it in your log while it's still listed and the bonus is paid on top of the quest's normal reward, once. The board shows carrying it or bonus paid next to each one. "Days" here count the server's running time, not the in-game sun.

Warden Aldric's Bounty Board

Once you've finished Aldric's second story quest, his menu gains a [📜 Bounty Board] button with three new bounties every in-game day:

Kind Example
Slay a kind of monster Slay 15 Skeletons
Slay Magic or Rare monsters Slay 3 Magic or Rare monsters
Bring items Bring 16 Bones
Slay a mini-boss Slay 1 mini-boss
  • [Accept] a bounty, then [Abandon] it if you change your mind (its progress is lost).
  • Kill bounties count as you go. Bring-item bounties check your bags when you turn them in.
  • Turn finished ones in to Aldric. They pay coins (2% more per character level), XP, and the harder ones can also pay gear.

The quest log

Open it with [📜 Quests] on the character sheet. It lists every quest you're carrying, the stage it's on and its count:

Button Does
[★ Claim] Shown when a side or world quest is done. Nothing pays out by itself - click it to get the reward
[Track] Puts an arrow and the distance to the quest's goal above your hotbar
[Hide tracker] Clears the tracker
[✖] Drops a quest
[↻ Refresh] Re-checks every quest's progress and redraws the log
[✦ Encounters] The encounter you're in, and the ones you've finished
  • Finished story quests show ready: turn it in to Warden Aldric instead of a claim button.
  • You can carry up to 85 side quests at once. Story quests don't count. The All tab groups them under Story, Combat, Gathering and Crafting headings.
  • A ✔ Finished section at the bottom lists everything you've completed. Hover any quest name for its goals and story.

What quests ask of you

Each quest has one or more stages, done in order. Common kinds:

  • Slay monsters - any monster, a kind, Magic or better, sometimes only inside a certain area.
  • Collect items - have them in your bags. Some quests take them, and they're taken the moment the stage is complete, even right when you accept. A message tells you when that happens.
  • Bring items to a person, or talk to someone.
  • Reach a place, or find several spots in any order.
  • Earn XP in a gathering stat (counted from when you accepted), reach a character level, craft or forge an item.
  • Clear a dungeon at a tier or higher, or take part in an encounter.

Some stages have conditions, like only at night, and pause while the condition isn't met. Others have a rule (for example don't die). Breaking it starts that stage over, and a message tells you why.

Rewards

Quests can pay any mix of Gold Coins, character XP, stat XP, attribute or perk points, rolled gear (sized to your level), named items, dungeon keys, forge ingredients and modifiers, Blacksmithing XP, and even a mount. The reward is shown on the offer's hover. Repeatable quests pay less, and some of their rewards are random.

Playing in a party

Party members share quest progress: kills, places reached, spots found, people talked to and encounters finished all count for every member carrying the same quest. Items you hand in or collect are your own. See Parties.
