Skill trees

How the class perk trees work, including perk points, the trunk, the three branches, notables and keystones, the shared Trades nodes, refunds and resets.

The perk tree

Every class has its own perk tree. You spend perk points on it to shape your build: more damage for one ability, a tougher body, faster mana, or a keystone that changes how you play.

  • Open it with [✦ Perk Tree] on your character sheet (right-click your Exile’s Journal), or with [Passive Tree] in the level-up message.
  • Points: you get 1 perk point per character level (see Leveling). This is on top of the attribute point each level gives you for the stat sheet.
  • Before you pick a class you only see the bottom row (the Origin and the shared Trades nodes). Any points you don't spend wait for your class tree.
  • The tree icons come from the resource pack.

The shape

Every class tree is drawn the same way. Only the names and effects change:

K-o-o-o-N N-o-K-o-N N-o-o-o-K | | | | N-o-N-o-o o-N-o-N-o o-o-N-o-N | | | | | | | o-o-E-o-N o-o-E-o-o N-o-E-o-o | | | o---------o---------o | o | N-N-N-N-N-N-S-N-N-N-N

  • S, the Origin, sits on the bottom row. You always own it.
  • The trunk climbs from the Origin. Every class has the same trunk:
Node Type Effect
Steady Breath small +20 max mana.
Light Feet small (the hub) +4% move speed.
Thick Hide small (left of the hub) +4 max health.
Second Breath notable (right of the hub) +25% mana regeneration.
  • The three branches are the grids on top. Each is 3 rows of 5 nodes. The left branch is entered from Thick Hide, the middle from Light Feet and the right from Second Breath, always through the bottom-middle node (E). Inside a branch there are several routes and loops. It holds 4 notables, and the keystone sits in the far top corner, six steps from the entry.
  • The Trades run left and right of the Origin on the bottom row (see below).

Node types

Symbol Type Cost What it gives
● Small 1 point +2 bonus levels in one of the branch's stats (+1 for Might). Like gear bonuses, these can go past the cap of 50 but don't unlock perks or passives.
◆ Notable 1 point A named effect, often an upgrade to one of your abilities.
★ Keystone 3 points A big build-defining effect with a real downside.

You can take any node that touches a node you own.

How far points go

  • One branch is 15 nodes and costs 17 points (10 small, 4 notables, 1 keystone). The trunk costs 4.
  • The fastest keystone is in the middle branch: 2 trunk nodes + 6 branch nodes + the 3-point keystone = 11 points, so character level 12 (you earn your first point at level 2). A side branch needs one more trunk node (level 13).
  • By level 40 you have 39 points: the trunk and about two full branches. All three branches take 55 points, so for most of your climb you have to choose.

The Trades

The Trades are shared by every class and kept when you change class. They form two chains that start next to the Origin. Each node costs 1 point, and you take them in order outwards from the Origin. You can buy Trades nodes even before you have a class.

Gathering chain (to the left of the Origin):

Order Node Effect
1 Green Hands +10% bonus chance on every gathering skill.
2 Lucky Find +1 luck.
3 Stonecutter Haste I while holding a pickaxe.
4 Angler's Luck +2 luck while holding a fishing rod.
5 Bountiful Life Double training XP for Mining, Logging, Farming, Fishing and Cooking.
6 Master Chef Eating cooked food gives a random 30 s buff: Speed, Haste, Strength or Regeneration.

Blacksmithing chain (to the right of the Origin):

Order Node Effect
1 Forge Hand +1 gem roll on every piece you forge.
2 Steady Hammer +1 affix slot on every piece you forge.
3 Thrifty Combining at the forge takes 8, not 9.
4 Quick Kiln Blacksmithing XP +25%.

See Forge for what gem rolls and affix slots mean.

Refunds and resets

  • Taking back one node is free, but only a node at the end of your path (one that touches just one other node you own). That way nothing you own gets cut off. In a loop, you unpick from one end. Click an owned node to refund it.
  • [Reset tree] refunds your whole class tree and your Trades. It costs 25 gold coins per character level (level 20 = 500 coins). It is free while you own only one node.
  • Changing class refunds every point in your class tree for free. The new class starts with an empty tree, and your Trades nodes stay. See Classes.

The stat sheet's [↺ Respec] is a different thing: it refunds attribute points, not perk points. See Stats.
