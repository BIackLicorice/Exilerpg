Stats

All 16 stats, with what each level gives, the level cap, how each one trains, the perks they unlock, and the passives you choose.

How stats work

Exile RPG has 16 stats. Each one has its own level and levels up by doing its thing. You get stronger at what you actually do: fight in melee and Might rises, sprint and Swiftness rises, mine and Mining rises.

  • Level = trained levels + attribute points you spent on it, up to the cap of 50.
  • Attribute points: every character level gives you 1 attribute point. Spend it on any stat with [+1] or [+5] on the character sheet or on the stat's own page. A spent point is +1 level right away. (You also get 1 perk point per character level, for your perk tree.)
  • Bonus levels (shown in aqua as +N) come from gear, sets, amulets, your perk tree and Herald buffs. They add to the stat's effect and can go past the cap. They don't unlock perks or passives and don't count towards prestige.
  • What a stat does = (level + bonus levels) × its per-level value.
  • Stat effects, perks and passives only apply while you hold a real weapon (sword, axe, mace, trident, bow, crossbow or Exile RPG weapon). Bare-handed or holding a tool, you punch for exactly 1 heart.

Open your character sheet by right-clicking your Exile’s Journal. Each stat has one row with its level, bonus levels, prestiges (✦), an XP bar, [+1] / [+5] when you have points, ★ when a passive is ready to choose, and MAX [✦] at the cap. Click a stat's name to open its page: its effect, how to train it, its perks and its passives. [✚ Bonuses] lists every bonus you have and where it comes from.

Blacksmithing mastery: levels 1-10 forge Common (copper), 11-21 Uncommon (iron), 22-32 Rare (gold), 33-43 Mythic (diamond), 44-50 Legendary (netherite). Forge ingredients drop from mining, logging, fishing and hunting, and modifiers from monsters and ores. See Forge.

Gathering bonuses (Logging, Farming, Fishing, Cooking) roll per item. The chance equals the stat's total level in %, so gear bonus levels count too.

Training speed

The base training XP above is then adjusted:

  • Combat stats train at half speed: Vitality, Might, Ferocity, Precision, Arcana, Fortitude, Toughness and Steadfast.
  • Every stat except the five gathering stats and Blacksmithing (that is, the eight combat stats plus Swiftness and Fortune) also earns only two thirds of its XP.
  • Mining and Logging train at half pace.
  • Blacksmithing trains 25% slower.
  • Your class's scaling stats train 25% faster (see Classes).
  • Prestige multiplies a stat's training XP: ×2 after one prestige, ×3 after two, and so on (see Prestige).
  • Some nodes and passives double training XP: Bountiful Life (tree) for all five gathering stats, and the Geologist, Arborist, Green Thumb, Angler, Gourmand and Apprentice passives for their own stat.

XP needed for the next level = 20 + 8 × your current level. Level 0 to 1 needs 20 XP, and 49 to 50 needs 412. Might needs 1.5 times as much: 30 + 12 × level.

Leftover fractions are never lost. Small gains add up until they make a whole point. Training XP from every stat except the gathering stats and Blacksmithing also feeds your character level. See Leveling.

Respec

[↺ Respec] on the character sheet (or Warden Aldric's "Respec attributes") refunds every attribute point you spent. It costs 25 gold coins per character level. Your trained levels and passive choices are kept. Perks you only had thanks to spent points go away until you reach those levels again.

This only resets attribute points. Your perk tree is reset separately (see Skill trees).
