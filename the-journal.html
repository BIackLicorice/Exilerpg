Getting started

The Exile's Journal

The Exile's Journal opens your character sheet - what every line and button on it does.

The Exile's Journal is the glowing book every new character starts with. Right-click it and your character sheet opens in chat. Every menu in Exile RPG is a set of clickable chat buttons, and they all start here.

  • Lost it? Click [My journal] on the sheet and you get a new one. Nothing happens if you're already carrying one.
  • Near an anvil, right-clicking the journal opens the forge instead. Sneak while you right-click to open the sheet.
  • Most buttons show a hover text first. Hover before you click when you're unsure.

The character sheet, top to bottom

Header

  • Character Level with an XP bar and your XP toward the next level. See Leveling.
  • A points line: your unspent attribute points, perk points and tokens. Each part is clickable and opens its own page.
  • Your class button. Before level 5 it reads Class at Lv 5. At level 5 it becomes [★ Choose your class], and after that it shows your class and opens the class page. See Classes.

One row per stat

Each stat has its own row:

You see Meaning
The stat's name Click it to open the stat's own page (what it does, how it trains, its passives)
Lv and a small bar The stat's level and progress to its next level
[+1] / [+5] Spend 1 or 5 attribute points on this stat
Aqua +N Bonus levels from gear, amulets, set bonuses and your tree
★ A new stat passive is ready to choose - click it
MAX The stat is at its cap
[✦] Prestige this stat - see Prestige
✦N How many times you've prestiged this stat

All the details are on Stats.

The lines below the stats

These appear once they apply to you:

Line What it is
Wings Your wings and their tier, once you've earned them
Marks Story marks your choices have given you (hover for what they mean)
📖 Codex How many places, secrets and voices you've found. [Open the book] puts the Codex in your bag
🐾 Bestiary How many kinds of monster you've killed. [🐾 Bestiary] shows where each comes from, what it drops and its story. More of the story opens at 10, 100 and 1000 kills
🏆 Achievements Goals done, and the title you're wearing. [🏆 Achievements] and [👑 Titles] - see Titles
❔ [? Help] Every tutorial tip in one list. Click one to read it, or turn tips off and on
Encounter Shown while you're taking part in an encounter
Attribute Your Personal Attribute. Hover for its card, click for its page. See Attributes
📖 [My journal] A new journal if you lost yours

The footer buttons

Button Opens
[✚ Bonuses] Every bonus you have and where it comes from
[📜 Quests] Your quest log - see Quests
[👥 Party] The Party tab - see Parties
[✦ Perk Tree] Your class's perk tree - see Skill trees
[↺ Respec] Refund every attribute point you've spent, for coins (see below)
[↻] Redraw the sheet

Other menus you'll meet

  • Quest log - also opened from the Quest Board's [📜 Quest Log] button. It has an [✦ Encounters] page listing the encounter you're in and the ones you've finished.
  • Your Stable - sneak + right-click your Rider's Whistle. See Mounts.
  • NPC menus - right-click Warden Aldric, Brenna Ironhand, Hilde the Stablehand or the Quest Board. Stand within about 8 blocks, or their buttons won't work.

Respec of attribute points

[↺ Respec] on the sheet, or [↺ Respec attributes] at Warden Aldric, gives back every attribute point you've spent so you can spend them again. You confirm with a second click. Your trained levels and stat passive choices stay as they are.

The cost is 25 Gold Coins per character level (so 250 coins at level 10). Resetting your perk tree costs the same - see Skill trees.
