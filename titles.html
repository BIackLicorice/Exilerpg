Titles

Achievements, the titles they give, and how to wear a title above your head.

A title is a short phrase you can wear, shown above your head for everyone to see. You earn titles from achievements, from some deeds and encounters out in the world, and from setting dungeon records.

Achievements

Click [🏆 Achievements] on the character sheet. The page lists every goal in the game:

  • ✔ - earned, ○ - not yet.
  • Each line says what the goal takes and, if it gives one, its title.
  • Achievements are checked every 20 seconds. When you earn one, chat announces it with a sound, and its title is yours to wear.
  • Earned achievements stay earned.

The sheet's achievements line shows how many you've earned and the title you're wearing.

Dungeon records

Each dungeon keeps a record time per tier. Beat it and you hold the title Fleetfoot of that dungeon. Records can be beaten, and when someone beats yours the title passes to them and is taken off you automatically.

Wearing a title

Click [👑 Titles], on the character sheet or on the Achievements page:

  • Wearing: shows your current title.
  • Every title you hold is listed with [Wear].
  • [Take the title off] wears none.

You wear one title at a time. It floats on a line above your head, whether or not you're in a party.
