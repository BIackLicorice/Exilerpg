Classes & stats

Warrior

The Warrior fights with any sword, axe or mace and can deal heavy melee damage or tank, scaling with Might, Fortitude, Toughness, Vitality and Steadfast.

Role

The Warrior is a versatile damage dealer or tank. You slam the ground, leap into packs of monsters and harden your skin with Absorption. Build for raw damage with Might, or for staying power with Fortitude, Toughness, Vitality and Steadfast. A Warrior has the most scaling stats of any class.

Role Melee damage or tank
Casts with Any sword, axe or mace (vanilla ones too)
Starter weapon Warblade (if you carry no sword, axe or mace)
Armour Heavy: iron and chainmail looks. Warrior armour rolls the most armour of any class (×1.3).
Scales with (+25% training XP) Might, Fortitude, Toughness, Vitality, Steadfast
Keys 4: Right-click, Sneak + Right-click, F, Sneak + F
Subclasses (level 20) Paladin, Berserker

Weapons, armour and the shield

  • Every sword, axe and mace is a Warrior class weapon, from vanilla iron to rolled Exile RPG gear.
  • Warriors cannot block. Your weapon's right-click casts abilities instead.
  • Shield stance: a shield in your offhand gives +2 armour, +1 armour toughness and -15% damage. The damage penalty applies to melee and abilities alike.
  • Effects that say "with a shield in your offhand" or "blocking" trigger when a monster hits you while you hold a shield in your offhand. This includes Fortitude training, the Fortitude perks and passives, and the tree's Shield Master and Iron Fortress. Falls, fire and other damage don't count.
  • Warrior gear rolls mostly Fortitude, Toughness, Vitality, Might and Steadfast. It rarely rolls Ferocity, Precision or Arcana.
  • The Warrior’s Boon amulet has a passive that only works for Warriors. See Amulets.

See Weapons and Armour for the full lists.

Abilities

Damage is a base number plus a share of your stat levels: "5 + 40% Might + 20% Steadfast" at Might 30 and Steadfast 20 deals 5 + 12 + 4 = 21. Hover an ability on the class page to see your numbers. See Abilities for how keys, mana and cooldowns work.

Key Ability Mana Cooldown What it does Damage
Right-click Ground Slam 20 6 s Slam the ground. Hostile mobs within 6 blocks are hit and knocked into the air. 5 + 40% Might + 20% Steadfast
Sneak + Right-click Crashing Slam 15 8 s Leap forward and crash down, damaging hostile mobs within 5.5 blocks of where you land. No fall damage. 6 + 40% Might + 20% Toughness
F Iron Skin 25 20 s Absorption for 10 s: 4 + 20% Toughness + 20% Fortitude health, rounded down to whole Absorption levels (4 health, 2 hearts, each). none
Sneak + F Ultimate Unlocks with your subclass.

Subclasses

Subclass Upgrade Ultimate (Sneak + F)
Paladin (holy hybrid) Consecrate replaces Ground Slam (25 mana, 8 s): a slam for 5 + 40% Might + 20% Vitality that blesses the ground for 4 s. Enemies on it take 2 + 10% Might + 10% Vitality every half second, and players on it get Regeneration II. Divine Judgment (50 mana, 40 s): holy light strikes every hostile mob within 10 blocks for 12 + 60% Might + 40% Vitality, and heals you and nearby players.
Berserker (raw damage) Savage Slam replaces Crashing Slam (15 mana, 8 s): a harder Crashing Slam for 8 + 50% Might + 25% Ferocity. Landing gives you Strength I for 5 s. Bloodrage (40 mana, 40 s): an 8 s rage with Strength I and Speed I. Every melee hit deals 2 + 15% Might + 15% Ferocity bonus damage and heals you a little.

Perk tree

Every class tree has the same shape: a shared trunk and three branches, each ending in a keystone. How points, refunds and the Trades nodes work is on Skill trees. Small nodes give bonus stat levels. They work like gear bonuses: they can go past the cap, but they don't unlock perks. Might small nodes give +1 level instead of +2.

Juggernaut

The left branch, entered from Thick Hide. Small nodes: 4 × +2 Toughness, 3 × +2 Fortitude, 3 × +2 Vitality.

Node Type Cost Effect
Bulwark Notable 1 +1.5 armour.
Stone Skin Notable 1 +1 armour toughness.
Shield Master Notable 1 A monster's hit on your shield heals you 1 heart.
Iron Resolve Notable 1 Iron Skin lasts 15 s (was 10).
Iron Fortress ★ Keystone 3 With a shield in your offhand: no knockback, projectiles hit you for 30% less, and monster hits you take deal 3 damage to every monster within 4 blocks. -0.5 attack speed. Crashing Slam is gone from your keys.

Good to know

  • The Berserker branch and the Berserker subclass share a name, but you don't have to take both. Every branch works with every subclass. Hallowed and Judgment only help a Paladin.
  • Iron Fortress also locks out Savage Slam, the Berserker's version of Crashing Slam.
  • Reckless and Iron Resolve stack. Iron Skin then lasts about 8 s.
  • Fortitude trains from monster hits you take with a shield in your offhand, Toughness from big hits (3+ hearts), Vitality from any damage you survive and Steadfast from hostile mobs hitting you. A Warrior who tanks levels up their defences fast. See Stats.
